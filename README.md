What I learned:
- Modern AI research began in the 1950s, when it decoded a German communications to help put an end to the World War II.
  
- Generative AI is the newest form of Aritifical Intelligence introduced to the public.
  
- There are 3 different types of ai (A)ritifical (N)arrow (I)ntelligence, (A)rtificial (G)eneral (I)ntelligence, and (A)rtificial (S)uper (I)ntelligence. Each one is seperated by their level of complexity.
  
- Machine learning is a subset of AI.
  
- Conversational AI tools are one application of Generative AI. They generate text, images, video or audio based on text given to it.
  
- Inaccuracies in AI are called "hallucinations".
  
- A training set is a collection of data used by (M)achine (L)earning to teach AI.
  
- AI Augumentation is the AI acting as an assistant to the human. AI automation is the AI replacing the role of the human.
  
- Notebook LM is for studying and research. It summarizes PDFs or large files so you can grasp concepts in a faster time.
  
- I learned that my LockIn App and ChatGPT are AI tools (similar to a car). GPT-5.5 is my AI model (similar to a mototr) and OpenAI API would be how my tool and model commuicate (similar to a road).
